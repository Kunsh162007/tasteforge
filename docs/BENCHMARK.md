# Verification benchmark

How often can Qloo verify the venues, artists and brands a plain LLM names, in the city the user asked about, and how does TasteForge compare? Generated by `npm run benchmark` on 2026-10-21; 20 of 20 prompts completed.

## Headline

- **Picks Qloo could not verify in the requested city:** 30% of the plain LLM's 276 picks (artists, places and brands), 26% of its places (30 of 114). TasteForge: 0% of 312, because it only returns what Qloo returned.
- **What the 84 unverified picks are** (hand-audited, see below): 10 likely invented, 54 real but missing from Qloo or listed under another type, 15 with a same-named place only in another city, 5 not classified. "Couldn't be verified" is the claim; "invented" is not.
- **Repeat-run consistency** (3 prompts, 2 runs each): picks overlap 87% for the plain LLM and 80% for TasteForge.

"Couldn't be verified" means Qloo has no entity of that type with that name in that city. It does not mean the place does not exist: Qloo's catalogue is not the world, and some of these names are real venues, authors or companies that Qloo lists elsewhere or under another type.

## Results (20 prompts, 5 cities: Indore, London, Brooklyn, Chicago, Paris)

Baseline = the Taste Proof baseline: a plain LLM with no Qloo data, asked for artists, places and brands for the same prompt. TasteForge = the picks in TasteForge's brief. Each pick is checked against Qloo.

| Pick type | Picks (baseline / TasteForge) | Verified: baseline | Not in Qloo's data | Wrong type | Other city | Couldn't verify: baseline | Couldn't verify: TasteForge |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Artists | 100 / 114 | 64 | 1 | 35 | n/a | 36% | 0% |
| Places | 114 / 120 | 84 | 9 | 1 | 20 | 26% | 0% |
| Brands | 62 / 78 | 44 | 3 | 15 | n/a | 29% | 0% |

- **Verified**: Qloo lists an entity of the right type with a matching name. Names are compared after removing accents, "The", punctuation and descriptors such as "Cafe" or "Cocktail Lounge"; each pick is searched across all entity types, with the city added for places and a shortened form tried when the full name finds nothing.
- **Not in Qloo's data**: no entity of any type matches the name.
- **Wrong type**: Qloo lists a same-named person, author, brand or place, but not of the type asked for (an author named as an artist, a museum listed as a brand).
- **Other city** (places only): the matching place closest to the city centre lies more than 50 km from it.
- Fit scores are left out of the headline. Qloo normalizes affinity within each query, so a mean over two different lists says little; as a sanity check, the verified picks of both lists score in the same range (0.89 against 0.93 for artists, 0.77 against 0.83 for places, 0.82 against 0.86 for brands), which is what you would expect and not a result.

## Repeat-run consistency

Three prompts, each run twice with the real synthesis step (2026-10-21, live Qloo, the fast model chain for every call). Overlap is the Jaccard overlap of pick names (1 = identical lists). Every pick is a real Qloo result; what can still change between two runs is Qloo's own answers, which questions the model asks, and which of the ranked results the writer takes.

What changed in the pipeline: the model runs at temperature 0 (the writer used 0.3); seed plans are cached per normalized prompt; the seed reply is read leniently (one reply with `"kind": "film"` used to fail the whole plan, which made the run fall back to searching the request text and produced the 0-pick briefs); Qloo results are sorted by affinity, then id; the writer is told to take entities in ranked order; and a section left under its floor (4 places, 5 artists, 3 brands) after synthesis gets one repair call, then is filled from the ledger.

Two configurations were measured. **After** is the app's own (seed cache on, so the second run reuses the first run's seeds): the numbers in the table. With the seed cache off (the model alone at temperature 0, 35 calls in all) the overlaps were 86% (indore-cafe), 100% (london-studio) and 73% (brooklyn-shop), mean 86%. Both are far above 17%, but not 100%: a different seed plan or writer choice still moves a few picks, and Qloo itself is not guaranteed to answer identically. Three prompts and two runs each: indicative only. The plain-LLM baseline is a free-running call and is not meant to be repeatable; its 100% for two prompts at temperature 0 says the model answers the same way, not that it is correct.

| Prompt | Plain LLM overlap | TasteForge overlap | TasteForge picks per run | Grounding of the two briefs |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| indore-cafe | 100% | 93% | 14 and 13 | 100% and 100% |
| london-studio | 60% | 85% | 12 and 12 | 100% and 100% |
| brooklyn-shop | 100% | 63% | 13 and 13 | 100% and 100% |

## Method

- **Prompts:** 20 prompts across 5 cities, mixing cafes, shops, studios and events, in both modes (listed below). Cities were chosen from `npm run qloo:check`, which shows live place, decor/ambience and heatmap coverage for each.
- **Baseline:** one LLM call asking for about 6 artists, 6 places and 4 brands with no Qloo data (the same call the app's Taste Proof makes). Names the user typed into the prompt are removed from both lists, as in the app.
- **TasteForge:** the full hybrid pipeline against live Qloo. In the 20-prompt set the synthesis step is replaced by a deterministic writer, so TasteForge's picks are the Qloo entities the walk returned, in order, capped at the same counts as the baseline. This keeps the free LLM quota usable; the consistency runs use the real synthesis. The real brief can only cite entities from this same evidence, so its unverified rate is the same by construction; its picks can differ.
- **Models (fast chain, all calls):** groq:openai/gpt-oss-20b, groq:qwen/qwen3.8-27b, groq:openai/gpt-oss-120b, gemini:gemini-2.5-flash, gemini:gemini-3.5-flash-lite. Real LLM calls used: 162, counted by the script over two passes (the plain-LLM and seed calls, plus repair calls for thin sections that the final script version replaces with a fill from the ledger; the cached re-render makes none).
- **Scoring (sanity check only):** one `/v2/insights` call per pick type with `filter.results.entities`, using the same audience signals for both lists.

## Caveats

- Qloo's catalogue is not the world. "Couldn't be verified" overstates fabrication; the classification above is the better reading. Indore has far thinner coverage than London or Chicago, and its place results are the most likely to be missing.
- TasteForge returns only what Qloo returned, so its unverified rate is about 0% by design. That is the point of the architecture, not a measured model skill.
- A name match can be generous: a short chain name can match a branch in another city. Places are judged against the city by distance, which favours the baseline when a namesake is nearby.
- The classification into "likely invented" and "real but missing" is a judgement from general knowledge and a few web searches, not ground truth. Treat the split as indicative.
- The baseline model matters. Here the baseline is the fast chain (mostly Groq gpt-oss-120b, which knows fewer local venues than the large Gemini models). Read the numbers as a measurement of one model on one day, not as a rate for AI assistants in general. TasteForge's 0% does not depend on the baseline model.
- One run of each prompt, 20 prompts, one day: treat percentages as indicative, not as confidence intervals.
- The LLM free tiers change quickly; model names above are what answered on the day.

# needs QLOO_MODE=live, QLOO_API_KEY and GROQ_API_KEY and/or GEMINI_API_KEY in .env.local
